## 📖 Project Overview

**Joker Poker Board** - casino poker game built for Stake Engine platform.

### Game Concept:
- **5-card board-only poker** (no hole cards, like Texas Hold'em board)
- **Up to 2 Jokers** as wild cards (appear in 17.86% of rounds)
- **13 hand types** from High Card to Royal Flush
- **Optimal Joker resolution** - always substitutes for maximum payout
- **9 bet levels** from $0.10 to $1,000
- **Max win:** $400,000
- **Validated RTP:** 98.34%

### Why Stake Engine?
Traditional casino game setup requires:
- ❌ Node.js backend
- ❌ PostgreSQL/Redis
- ❌ Custom RNG
- ❌ Wallet API
- ❌ Session management

**Stake Engine provides:**
- ✅ Pre-generated outcomes (fairness guaranteed)
- ✅ RGS (Remote Game Server) infrastructure
- ✅ Wallet & session management
- ✅ CDN hosting
- ✅ TypeScript client library

**Result:** We only build game math (Python) + frontend (TypeScript)

## 📊 Game Math (Validated)

### Paytable - RTP: 98.34%

| Hand Type | Ranks | Multiplier | Frequency | Contribution |
|-----------|-------|------------|-----------|--------------|
| **Royal Flush** | 10-J-Q-K-A same suit | x1000 | 0.003% | High impact |
| **Straight Flush** | 5 consecutive same suit | x100 | 0.017% | High impact |
| **Four of a Kind** | 4 same rank | x40 | 0.307% | Medium impact |
| **Full House** | 3 + 2 same rank | x20 | 0.305% | Medium impact |
| **Flush** | 5 same suit | x10 | 0.360% | Medium impact |
| **Straight** | 5 consecutive ranks | x5 | 1.119% | Medium impact |
| **Three (High)** | 3x J/Q/K/A | x3.0 | 3.7% | Medium impact |
| **Three (Low)** | 3x 2-10 | x1.8 | 3.7% | Medium impact |
| **Two Pair (High)** | 2 pairs with J+ | x1.5 | 2% | Low impact |
| **Two Pair (Low)** | 2 pairs 2-10 | x0.8 | 2% | Low impact |
| **Pair (High)** | 2x J/Q/K/A | x0.9 | 22% | **Major impact** |
| **Pair (Low)** | 2x 2-10 | x0.55 | 23% | **Major impact** |
| **High Card (High)** | J/Q/K/A | x0.35 | 17% | **Major impact** |
| **High Card (Low)** | 2-10 | x0.1 | 24% | **Major impact** |

### Key Statistics:
- **Total hands tested:** 1,000,000
- **RTP:** 98.34% (target 97% ±1%)
- **Joker frequency:** 17.86% (theoretical: 17.82%)
- **High-value hands (Flush+):** 1.3%
- **Medium hands (Straight to Three):** 16.5%
- **Common hands (Pair + High Card):** 82.2%

### Joker Mechanics:
- **Probability:** 17.86% of rounds have at least 1 Joker
- **Math:** With 2 Jokers in 54-card deck, P(≥1 Joker in 5 cards) = 1 - C(52,5)/C(54,5) = 17.82%
- **Resolution:** Brute-force optimal substitution
  - 1 Joker: Test 48 possible cards → pick max payout
  - 2 Jokers: Test 2,256 combinations → pick max payout
- **Always player-favorable:** Joker becomes whatever gives highest payout

## 🏗️ Architecture Decision (This Session)

### Question: Python Math SDK - отдельный репозиторий?

**Evaluated 3 options:**

#### ❌ Option 1: Separate standalone repo
- Create `pokerspin-math` from scratch
- Build own infrastructure (Makefile, upload scripts, etc.)
- **Rejected:** Too much work, reinventing wheel

#### ❌ Option 2: Monorepo (Python + TypeScript)
- Add `math_sdk/` to current `pokerspin` repo
- Keep everything together
- **Rejected:** Mixing languages, complex CI/CD

#### ✅ Option 3: Fork official Stake Engine Math SDK
- Fork https://github.com/StakeEngine/math-sdk
- Add our game to `games/joker_poker/`
- Use their infrastructure (Makefile, tools, etc.)
- **CHOSEN:** Best of both worlds!

### Why Option 3 Won:
1. ✅ **Ready infrastructure** - build system, validation, testing
2. ✅ **Guaranteed compatibility** - their framework, their way
3. ✅ **Easy updates** - sync from upstream when needed
4. ✅ **Separation of concerns** - Math SDK separate from frontend
5. ✅ **One-time use** - Math SDK only needed for outcome generation
